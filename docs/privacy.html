Privacy Policy — Rockerless

The canonical copy of this policy lives at bytecrest.dev/rockerless/privacy.

Last updated 12 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app dev.bytecrest.rockerless

Rockerless is a volume control utility for Android made by Bytecrest. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with information. The short version: nothing leaves your phone, because the app has no way to send it.

We do not collect any data

Rockerless does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs.

No internet access

The app does not request the INTERNET permission in its Android manifest. Without that permission the Android operating system prevents the app from opening a network connection at all, so there is no channel through which data could be sent anywhere, even in principle.

What is stored on your device

Your preferences are saved in the app's private storage on your phone and are never uploaded. They are limited to:

  • Bubble size, opacity, and whether it hides when idle.
  • The screen edge and vertical position you last dragged the bubble to.
  • Whether the bubble was switched on, so it can be restored after a reboot.
  • The media volume level remembered for the unmute action.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions and why they exist

  • Draw over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — shows the floating bubble above other apps. Granted by you in system settings.
  • Modify audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS) — mutes and restores media volume and opens the system volume panel.
  • Foreground service — keeps the bubble available while you use other apps, with a visible notification.
  • Notifications — shows the required notification while the bubble is running, including a Stop button.
  • Start at boot — restores the bubble after a restart, only if you had it switched on.
  • Vibrate — haptic feedback when you long-press to mute.

Rockerless does not use an accessibility service, does not read the contents of your screen or other apps, and does not access your contacts, files, camera, microphone, or location.

Sharing

There is nothing to share. No data is collected, so no data is disclosed to third parties. No advertising or analytics providers are involved.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Rockerless ever needs to handle data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
